# Privacy Policy — Auto Reels Uploader **Last updated: October 11, 2026** ## 1. Overview Auto Reels Uploader is a privately operated tool intended to help its operator manage Facebook Pages and publish video content to Pages they are authorized to manage. ## 2. Information Used Depending on the permissions granted through Facebook Login, the application may process the Facebook account name and ID, the names and IDs of Pages managed by the account, Page access tokens, and information necessary to publish content to those Pages. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information is used only to authenticate the account, identify authorized Pages, maintain the application's local account list, and perform publishing actions requested by the operator. ## 4. Local Storage and Security Account information and access tokens are stored on the operator's Windows computer. The application uses Windows data protection features to encrypt locally stored account data. No computer storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. ## 5. Sharing and Disclosure The application operator does not intentionally sell personal information or disclose stored access tokens to third parties. Facebook processes information according to its own terms and privacy policies. Information may be transmitted to Meta when required to authenticate or perform requested publishing actions. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion The operator can remove locally stored account information by deleting it through the application when that feature is available, or by removing the application's local data files. Revoking Facebook permissions or access tokens may also be necessary to end access to Facebook resources. ## 7. User Rights and Contact For questions about this application or requests concerning information processed by its operator, contact the application operator through the contact information provided by the operator. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change.
